Sky Stack · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Operator: Touch Melody Inc

This Privacy Policy explains how Touch Melody Inc (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with Sky Stack, including the same mini game where it is listed as Wildrise (the “Game”), and the related legal and support pages we provide. It does not describe unrelated games or services operated by other businesses.

The current Game stores progress and preferences locally. It does not require a separate Touch Melody player account or offer in-app purchases. It runs inside TikTok and uses TikTok’s advertising and revisit features. Those platform services involve their own information processing, described below.

For privacy questions or requests, contact alex@touchmelody.top.

1. Information involved

Local game information

The Game saves your best score, highest tower, language choice, sound setting, and the status of one-time revisit rewards and unused starting-width passes in the storage provided by your device, browser, or TikTok mini game environment. This information is used on that device. The current Game does not send these saved records to a separate Touch Melody game server or automatically synchronize them between devices.

TikTok platform and advertising information

TikTok provides the account environment, game container, advertising delivery, and shortcut and profile-revisit services. When you use these features, TikTok and the providers involved in delivering its services may process platform or account identifiers, device and software information, network information such as IP address, game or feature interactions, ad requests, ad viewing and completion information, and diagnostic information. The exact information depends on the service, your region, your settings, and applicable permissions.

The Game receives results needed to operate these features, such as whether an ad was completed or whether a revisit reward is available. We may also receive platform-provided usage, advertising, revenue, or diagnostic reports to operate and support the Game. Information made available through TikTok is subject to the applicable platform arrangements and privacy notices.

Information you choose to send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. A support report may include a device model, app version, screenshot, or error description. Please do not send passwords or payment credentials.

Visits to our legal pages

These legal pages do not include advertising scripts or analytics cookies added by us. Website hosting services, including GitHub Pages when used to host these pages, may process standard connection and request information to deliver the pages, maintain security, and meet their own legal obligations.

Information the current Game does not request directly

You do not need to provide a name, contact details, precise location, contact list, photographs, camera access, or microphone access directly to Touch Melody to play the current Game. This statement does not mean that TikTok stops processing information associated with your TikTok account or use of its platform.

2. Why information is used

Information described in this policy is used, as relevant, to:

  • Run the Game, remember local preferences, and display your local records.
  • Determine whether a requested ad reward or revisit reward is available, grant the stated benefit, and reduce duplicate or abusive claims.
  • Deliver advertising through TikTok and understand the performance of the Game and its monetization features.
  • Respond to support requests, diagnose errors, and improve reliability and usability.
  • Protect the Game and users, address misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis for processing we control, the basis depends on the activity. It may include providing the requested service, legitimate interests such as maintaining reliability and preventing abuse where permitted, compliance with law, or consent where required. TikTok’s own privacy notices explain the bases for TikTok’s processing.

3. TikTok and service providers

The Game is a mini game accessed through TikTok. TikTok controls its account system, ad delivery, platform permissions, and other platform functions. TikTok’s processing is explained in the privacy policy that applies to your region. You can access it in TikTok’s privacy settings or consult TikTok’s Privacy Policy.

Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose to watch one, TikTok processes the ad interaction and returns a completion or error result to the Game. Ad personalization, where available, depends on TikTok’s settings, consent status, and applicable law. We do not operate a separate advertising network inside this version of the Game.

Information may be handled by providers used for platform distribution, advertising, website hosting, email, or support. Such providers may process information to deliver services to us, or for their own purposes as explained in their applicable notices. For example, the hosting provider’s information practices are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We may disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or carry out a business transfer, subject to applicable requirements. This policy does not give us unrestricted access to your TikTok account or personal profile.

4. Storage, deletion, and retention

Local records and settings: These remain in the relevant local storage until they are changed, removed, or cleared. Uninstalling an app, clearing TikTok or browser data, switching devices, or changes in the platform’s storage may remove them. The current Game does not provide a separate cloud backup of these records.

Support correspondence: We retain information you send us for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, handle related issues, maintain appropriate records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and applicable requirements.

Platform and hosting information: TikTok and other providers retain information under the policies and settings applicable to their services. Clearing a local game record does not by itself delete information held independently by TikTok or a hosting provider.

5. Your choices and privacy rights

  • You can change the Game’s sound and language settings in the Game.
  • You can choose not to watch a rewarded ad. The associated ad benefit will not be granted, and you can use the non-ad restart option.
  • You can manage TikTok account, advertising, and privacy settings in TikTok, and device permissions in your device settings.
  • You can remove locally stored game data using the relevant browser, TikTok, or device controls. This may also remove your scores, preferences, and local reward history.

Depending on where you live and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising, sale, or sharing activities. You may also have a right to complain to a competent data-protection authority.

To make a request about information handled by Touch Melody, email alex@touchmelody.top. Describe your request without sending unnecessary sensitive information. We may request proportionate information to verify and respond to it, and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. For information controlled independently by TikTok, use TikTok’s privacy request tools or contact TikTok; we cannot directly delete records that we do not control.

6. Age requirements

Access to the Game through TikTok is subject to TikTok’s account eligibility, regional age requirements, and any additional requirements under applicable law. The current Game does not ask players to submit contact details or create a separate Touch Melody account to play.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, contact us at the address below. We will review the request and take appropriate steps for information we control. Requests about a TikTok account or TikTok’s own processing should also be directed to TikTok.

7. International processing

The Game is distributed through a platform used in multiple countries. TikTok, hosting, email, or other providers may process information in jurisdictions different from your own, where laws may differ. Their relevant notices describe their international processing practices. Where we arrange an international transfer of personal information we control, we will apply safeguards required by applicable law.

8. Security

We seek to use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information and services involved. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect your TikTok account and device, and report suspected Game-related privacy or security issues using our contact address.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, our practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date identifies the version posted here. Where required, we will provide an appropriate additional notice or obtain consent before applying a material change.
